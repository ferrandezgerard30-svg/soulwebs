Speed optimization

Fast sites rank better. And book more.

Google measures how quickly your pages load and respond — it calls these Core Web Vitals — and uses page experience in its rankings. Every site we build is tuned to pass them. Already have a website? We can speed it up too.

  • Images compressed to modern formats (WebP) and lazy-loaded
  • No heavy page builders or plugin bloat
  • Self-hosted fonts, minimal scripts
  • Global CDN with HTTPS
  • Layouts that don't jump while loading

Straight answers

Good questions.

Can you guarantee I'll be #1 on Google?

No — and nobody honestly can. Google decides rankings. What we promise is careful work by Google's rules, a fast site, and a clear monthly report showing where you're moving.

How long until my rankings improve?

In a quiet area, changes to your Google Business Profile and website can show within weeks. In competitive downtown areas, expect a few months of steady work.

I already have a website. Can you just make it faster?

Yes. We audit it in PageSpeed Insights, fix what slows it down (images, fonts, scripts, hosting) and tell you exactly what changed.

Do you work with US businesses from Spain?

Yes. Everything is done online, by email or video call, with invoices in US dollars.

Who owns my website and Google profile?

You do. Your Google Business Profile, domain and accounts stay in your name.

Where do you rank right now?

Send us your business name and city. We'll reply with a free snapshot of your Google rankings and site speed — no sales pitch.
