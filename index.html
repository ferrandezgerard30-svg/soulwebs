Why it matters.
Briefly.

Your next customer is searching right now. “Barber near me.” “Best tacos open now.” “Hair salon downtown.” They pick from the few businesses Google shows on the map — and most never scroll further.

Being there isn’t luck. It comes from a complete Google Business Profile, a fast website Google understands, steady reviews, and the same name, address and phone number everywhere online.

That’s what I do. Careful local SEO, done by Google’s rules, for businesses that already do great work — so more people find you, call you and walk in.

FAQ

Good questions.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work that helps people nearby find your business on Google search and Google Maps: your Google Business Profile, your website, your reviews and your business details across the web.

Can you guarantee I’ll be #1 on Google?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings, and Google itself warns against anyone who does. What I promise is careful work within Google’s rules and a clear monthly report showing what changed.

What does the free snapshot include?

A short look at how your business shows up on Google today: where you appear on Maps for a few key searches, gaps in your Google Business Profile and the main issues on your website. No commitment.

Do I need a new website?

Often not. Most local SEO starts with your Google Business Profile. A new website is only worth it if your current one is missing, broken or holding your rankings back.

Who owns my Google Business Profile and accounts?

You do. I work with manager access only. Your Google Business Profile, Search Console, Analytics and domain always stay in your name.

Do you work with businesses in the US from abroad?

Yes. All the work is done online. We talk by email, video call or WhatsApp, and you get invoices and payment links in US dollars.
