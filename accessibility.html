Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 5, 2026

I want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, zoom or other assistive technology.

What I aim for

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. That includes text alternatives for meaningful images, sufficient color contrast, a logical heading structure, visible keyboard focus, a “skip to content” link, and pages that work when zoomed to 200%.

Known limitations

Some screenshots of client websites are decorative or illustrative and may not describe every detail. Third-party websites linked from here (such as WhatsApp or Instagram) have their own accessibility.

Feedback

If something on this website is hard to use, please tell me at soulwebs2026@gmail.com. I’ll reply within 5 business days and, if needed, give you the information in another format.

Client websites

The websites I build for clients follow the same approach: alt text, readable contrast, keyboard navigation and labeled forms.
