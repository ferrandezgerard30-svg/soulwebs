Hair salons& barbershops

It's our specialty. We've spent years designing websites for salons — from neighborhood barbershops to upscale downtown salons.

We know how to organize a service menu, where to show prices (and when not to), how to make the gallery shine without slowing the site down, and which booking systems work at each volume.

We work with Fresha, Vagaro and Booksy. We also build custom booking systems (like we did for Nacho Huici) or send bookings straight to a call or text.

↗ In depth

Restaurants& hospitality

The restaurant challenge is clear: guests decide in under 30 seconds whether to book. The site has to load fast, show the menu without obstacles and let people reserve in one or two clicks.

We integrate with Resy and OpenTable for reservations, we connect DoorDash or Uber Eats for delivery, and we build bilingual menus when your guests speak more than one language.

Plus: food gallery, team, private events and group bookings. All designed so the restaurant speaks for itself.
