The short version: this website has no contact forms, no analytics, no advertising trackers and sets no cookies. I only get your personal data if you choose to contact me or hire me, and I use it only for that. I never sell it.

Owner: Soul Webs — the trade name of an independent professional based in Spain

Email: soulwebs2026@gmail.com

Website: soulwebsites.com

The owner of Soul Webs is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spain’s data protection law (LOPDGDD).

3. How long I keep it

Inquiries that don’t lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted. Client and invoicing records: as long as the law requires (in Spain, generally up to 6 years for accounting and tax purposes). Reviews: until you ask me to remove them. Hosting logs: as set by the hosting provider, usually a short period.

4. Who your data is shared with

I don’t sell or rent personal data. I only use trusted service providers that need it to do their job:

  • Vercel Inc. (USA) — website hosting.
  • Google (Gmail / Google Workspace) — email.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram) — only if you contact me there.
  • Stripe and/or PayPal — payment processing for clients.
  • An accountant or tax advisor (gestoría), and public authorities when the law requires it.

Some of these providers are in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to soulwebs2026@gmail.com; I’ll reply within one month. If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local data protection authority.

6. Visitors and clients in the United States

  • No sale or sharing: I do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, as those terms are defined in US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).
  • Your choices: wherever you live, you can ask me what personal information I have about you, ask me to correct or delete it, and I will not treat you differently for asking.
  • Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control: this website does not track you, so there is nothing to switch off.
  • Children: this website is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect their data.
  • Email: any marketing email I send identifies me, includes a physical address and an easy way to opt out, as required by the CAN-SPAM Act.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or similar technologies (such as tracking pixels or local storage) to track you. Fonts are hosted on this same server, so no data is sent to font providers. That is why you don’t see a cookie banner.

If you click a link to WhatsApp, Instagram or another website, that website may set its own cookies under its own policy. If I ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, I will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

8. Security

The website is served only over HTTPS, and I use two-factor authentication on the accounts that hold client data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Esta web no utiliza cookies ni tecnologías de seguimiento. Las fuentes se sirven desde el propio servidor. Por eso no hay banner de cookies. Si en el futuro se añade analítica u otras cookies no necesarias, se pedirá tu consentimiento antes.
