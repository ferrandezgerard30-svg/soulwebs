Booksy vs Fresha vs Vagaro: an honest comparison for barbershops

Three popular booking platforms for US salons, compared: monthly prices, new-client fees, pros and cons — with a summary table and a recommendation for your case.

📅 November 5, 2026 ⏱ 9 min read By Soul Webs

In the US, three of the most popular booking platforms for salons and barbershops are Booksy, Fresha and Vagaro. Each has a different pricing model, and the wrong choice can cost you real money in unnecessary fees every month.

We've connected booking platforms like these to real salon websites. This is the honest comparison we wish we'd read when we started. Prices are US list prices found in 2026 — always check the current ones before signing up.

Booksy: the barbershop favorite

Booksy is one of the most popular booking apps for US barbershops and salons. It has its own marketplace, a well-known client app and a monthly subscription.

How pricing works

Booksy charges a fixed monthly fee: $29.99/month for one user plus $20/month for each additional team member (US pricing, mid-2026). There's no commission on your regular bookings. A barbershop with 3 barbers pays about $70/month, plus card fees.

What's good about Booksy

  • No commissions on regulars: you pay the subscription and that's it. Great if you do a lot of bookings.
  • Mobile app that's polished for both you and your clients.
  • Automated marketing: reminders, birthday messages, no-show protection.
  • Well-known brand with clients, especially for barbershops.

The bad

  • You pay even in slow months. A quiet month costs the same as a busy one.
  • Getting promoted to new clients with Booksy Boost costs 30% of a qualifying new client's first visit.
  • With 5+ team members, the monthly bill adds up fast (over $100).

Who it's for

For salons with a steady base of regulars. If you do lots of bookings every month and your clients already know where to find you, the flat fee works out cheap.

Fresha: polished software, low monthly price

Fresha built its name as the "free" option. It now has paid plans, but it's still one of the cheapest choices for solo stylists.

How pricing works

Fresha costs $19.95/month for one bookable member on the Independent plan, or $14.95/month per bookable member on the Team plan (US pricing, fall 2026). On top of that:

  • A fee of 2.79% + $0.20 per online card payment (2.29% + $0.20 in person)
  • A fee of 20% on the first visit of each new client who finds you on the Fresha marketplace ($6 minimum)
  • Pay-per-use text and email marketing

The good

  • Low cost to start. Good if you're just opening.
  • Complete, well-designed software.
  • Clean, easy client app and calendar.

The bad

  • The new-client fees from the marketplace are high (20%).
  • Its marketplace is smaller than Booksy's in many US cities.
  • Card payments add processing fees on every transaction.

Who it's for

For solo stylists and new salons on a tight budget, or pros who mainly need a calendar and bring their own clients.

Vagaro: the all-in-one option

Vagaro is an all-in-one platform popular with US salons, spas and barbershops. Besides booking, it covers payments, forms, memberships, gift cards and its own consumer marketplace and app.

How pricing works

Vagaro charges a monthly subscription based on your number of bookable calendars. Its model is a flat fee per calendar: about $30/month for the first calendar and about $10/month for each additional one, according to 2026 pricing guides. Third-party guides report no new-client commission on its marketplace listing — confirm with Vagaro before you sign up.

The good

  • Predictable cost: no percentage taken from new clients.
  • Adding stylists is cheap compared with per-person pricing.
  • Lots of built-in tools: forms, memberships, packages, gift cards.

The bad

  • The number of features can feel overwhelming, and several add-ons cost extra each month.
  • Keyed-in and online card payments cost more than in-person ones.
  • Its marketplace may bring fewer new clients than Booksy in some areas, so don't count on it as your only source.

Who it's for

For salons that want one tool for everything and a predictable monthly bill, especially teams with several stylists.

The combination that works best

In practice, the salons doing best don't rely on a single platform. They combine:

  1. Their own website with direct booking (a booking button or a custom system) for clients who come from Google and word of mouth.
  2. Booksy, Fresha or Vagaro depending on whether they want the lowest monthly price or marketplace exposure.
  3. Instagram linking to their own website (not straight to Booksy) to build their brand.

This avoids paying marketplace fees on clients who would have found you anyway, and keeps the salon's brand front and center.

FAQ

How much does Booksy Boost really cost?

Booksy's US pricing lists Boost at 30% of a qualifying new client's first visit, with no charge on that client's later visits. Check the current terms in your Booksy account before turning it on.

Does Booksy take a cut of my regular bookings?

No. You pay the monthly subscription ($29.99 plus $20 per extra team member). If you take card payments through Booksy, standard processing fees apply (roughly 2.5–2.7% plus a few cents).

Can I use two platforms at once?

Yes, but keep one calendar as your main system and sync carefully to avoid double bookings.

Is a custom booking system on my website worth it?

If you pay a lot every month in platform fees and commissions, a custom system can pay for itself. We built one for Nacho Huici in Pamplona, Spain and it's the best investment in their website.

What happens if I leave Booksy, Fresha or Vagaro?

Most platforms let you export your client list, with some limits. That's why we always recommend your own website as home base, with your client relationships in your hands.
