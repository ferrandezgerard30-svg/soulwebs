Your own website or just Booksy/Fresha? what your salon really needs

The million-dollar question: is a Booksy profile enough, or do you need your own website? The short answer is "it depends" — and here's exactly on what.

📅 November 5, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read By Soul Webs

We hear it every week from salons and barbershops: "I already have Booksy and people book there — why do I need a website?" The short answer: it depends on what you want to achieve and how much you pay in fees. Here's the long answer.

What Booksy and Fresha do well

Credit where it's due — these platforms solve some things better than any website:

  • Calendar management for teams with several stylists
  • Mobile app where clients book in 30 seconds
  • Automatic reminders by text and email
  • No-show handling with clear policies
  • Marketplace that brings in new clients

If that's all you need, a small salon can run perfectly well on Booksy or Fresha without a website — especially if you've just opened and the budget is tight.

What a booking platform not can't do

But your own website does things no Booksy or Fresha profile can:

1. It puts you on Google when people search your name

When someone googles "Soul Hair Austin", the first result should be your website , not your Booksy page. With your own site, you control the first impression. Without one, someone else does.

2. It sets you apart

On Booksy every salon looks the same: same template, same buttons, same colors. On your website you choose the tone, the design, the photos and the words. Your brand is the only way out of "everyone looks the same".

3. It reaches people who don't use booking apps

Plenty of clients never install booking apps. They search Google, look at your website, then call or text. Without a website, they may never find you.

4. It can save you fees

A salon with its own website and direct booking doesn't pay marketplace fees on clients who find it directly. Marketplace features often charge a fee on new clients they bring you, so routing your own clients through your site keeps more money in your pocket.

The combination we recommend most

For most mid-size salons, this is what works best:

  1. Your own website as your brand's home base: homepage, services, gallery, reviews, blog.
  2. A "Book now" button on the site that opens Booksy (or Fresha, Vagaro, or a menu with several options).
  3. Instagram linking to your website (not straight to Booksy).
  4. Marketplace features ONLY for finding new clients, not as your main system.

This combination has three clear benefits:

  • Your brand comes first, not the platform's.
  • Showing up on Google with your own site improves local SEO.
  • If Booksy changes its prices one day, you don't lose your website or your clients.

When do you NOT need your own website?

There's a real case where Booksy or Fresha alone is enough:

  • You're a solo stylist or barber.
  • You rent a chair or suite inside someone else's salon.
  • All your clients come from Instagram or word of mouth.
  • You don't have the budget or interest for a website.

In that case, a strong Booksy profile plus a great Instagram can be enough. Your own website pays off once there's an established business behind it.

FAQ

Can my website connect to several booking platforms?

Yes. Booksy, Fresha and Vagaro offer official booking links or widgets for your website. Just keep one calendar as the source of truth to avoid double bookings.

If I have Booksy, does my website need its own calendar?

Not necessarily. The simplest option: a "Book now" button on your site that opens your Booksy page. The advanced option: embed Booksy's booking widget right on your site.

How soon will I notice a difference?

For your site to show up when people search your name: usually a few weeks. For new clients finding you through the site: typically a few months, depending on competition.

Does my neighborhood salon need a website?

Probably yes, even a simple one. Your clients search your name on Google, and if nothing shows up it looks like you've closed. A simple site (our Essential plan) covers that without overspending.

Can I move from Booksy to my own website without losing clients?

Yes, with a plan. Tell your regulars 1–2 months ahead, run both systems for about 3 months, and promote the new site on social media. Most regulars switch easily when they hear it from you.
