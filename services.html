What moves you up on Google.

Google ranks local businesses on three things: relevance, distance and prominence. This is the work we do on each one. All of it is part of the plan, and it can also work with the website you already have.

Google Business Profile
Categories, services, photos, posts and Q&A, kept up to date. Your profile is what shows in the Maps results, often before your website.
Local keywords
The searches your customers actually make, like “hair salon in [city]”, worked into your titles, headings, service pages and Schema.org.
Speed
Core Web Vitals in Google’s “good” range: compressed images, no page-builder bloat, minimal scripts. Fast for visitors, and Google rewards a good page experience.
Citations & reviews
The same name, address and phone on Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing and directories, plus a simple routine to ask every customer for a review, and reply to each one.
Monthly report
Where you rank, how many calls, direction requests and website clicks came from Google, in plain English. No jargon, no vanity numbers.
Tap any item to email us about it. Also available as add-ons: Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media and brand identity.
